# DEMO Principle: Reduce Choice To Increase Recognition

Status: DEMO / Candidate Principle
Date Created: DEMO
Last Updated: DEMO
Tags: DEMO, brand, recognition, system

## What I Believe

DEMO belief only:

A brand system should reduce weak variation when that variation makes recognition and decision-making harder.

## Why

People do not experience a brand system as an internal asset library. They experience it through moments of scanning, comparing, remembering, and choosing.

## Evidence

DEMO evidence only:

- `01_CASES/DEMO-case-flex-brew.md`
- `02_INSIGHTS/DEMO-clear-choice-beats-more-choice.md`

## Applies When

- The brand has too many similar assets.
- Product families are hard to distinguish.
- Visual variety is creating confusion rather than meaning.
- Recognition is more important than decorative richness.

## Does Not Apply When

- Variety is central to the brand promise.
- The audience expects exploration or collectability.
- The category needs expressive storytelling.
- Simplification would erase important product differences.

## How It Affects Design Decisions

Before adding new visual assets, first identify which cues already drive recognition and which variations are only adding noise.
